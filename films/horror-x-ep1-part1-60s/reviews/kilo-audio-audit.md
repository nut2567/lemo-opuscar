## Prioritized findings

### 1. Material SFX/action offset — coin_bounce (sc05, sc08) — ACTION REQUIRED
`sound-design.py:349` places coin_bounce at 40% into each scene (sc03/05/08). This is NOT synchronized to the actual coin action in the narration.

| Scene | SFX time | Narration coin action | Offset |
|-------|----------|----------------------|--------|
| sc03 | 12.46s | "เก็บ/เหรียญ/ใส่" (collect/coin/put) 12.79–13.43s | ~0.33s early |
| sc05 | 22.12s | "ตก" (fall) 23.31s; "กระทบ" (strike) 23.44s | ~1.2–1.3s early |
| sc08 | 35.81s | "หยอด" (insert coin) 36.93–37.45s | ~1.1s early |

sc05 and sc08 are material: the metallic bounce precedes the spoken fall/insert by over a second, so the SFX lands before the on-screen/narrated coin event. sc03 is minor (~0.33s).
Evidence: `sound-design.py:349` (`t_coin = sc["start"] + (sc["end"] - sc["start"]) * 0.4`) vs `voice-timeline.json` word timestamps and `script.json` sc05/sc08 direction (coin falling into tray / coin inserts into slot).
Recommendation: retime coin_bounce to the narration coin-contact word (sc05 ~23.4s, sc08 ~37.0s, sc03 ~13.0s) rather than fixed 40%.

### 2. Misleading QA claim — "Coin bounce sc03/05/08 placed at 40% into each scene" (SOUND-QA.md)
The claim is technically true (40% placement) but misleading: it reports placement compliance without verifying alignment to narration/action. A cue placed at 40% can still be materially early relative to the coin event (sc05/sc08). The QA table should note whether each SFX is action-synchronized, not just timeline-derived.
Evidence: `SOUND-QA.md` cue table rows + constraint row "✅ Placed at 40% into each scene"; mismatch vs narration timestamps above.

### 4. Intra-scene silence 0.566s in scene 10 — documented, acceptable

Evidence: `qa/narration-silence.txt`; `voice-timeline.json` scene 10 word timestamps.
