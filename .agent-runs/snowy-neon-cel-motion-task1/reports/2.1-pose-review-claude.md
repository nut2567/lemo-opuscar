## Defects

### D-1: Store→nun rootPath interpolation drift (CORRECTNESS)

**Location**: `rootPath` track, keys at t=4.4 and t=5.733.

**Issue**: The last cue-1 key (t=4.4) has `anchorWorld:[690,925]` (counter pose). The first cue-2 key (t=5.733) has `anchorWorld:[505,925]` (walk-start in the aisle). With track-level `interp:"smooth"`, the resolver smoothsteps between these over 1.333s. During the store shot's tail (frames 132–171), `anchorWorld.x` smoothly slides from 690 to ~505 — a ~185px world-space horizontal drift. At the store camera's zoom (~z=1.14 by shot end), this is ~211px screen-space over 39 frames (~5.4 px/frame), clearly visible even at the wide framing.

The scene-layout's shot-character-visibility confirms the store shot has verdict "VISIBLE — small figure at counter, fully in frame." The notes.md describes this as a "teleport" at the cut, but smooth interpolation makes it gradual, not instantaneous.

Additionally, `headingDeg` interpolates from 180 to 0 during this window, and `facing` (a step field) snaps from -1 to 1 at t=5.733 — creating a mid-rotation character who suddenly flips facing direction. `zone` similarly steps from "counter" to "shop" at t=5.733.

**Impact**: Visible character slide during the last ~1.3s of the store shot, contradicting the intended "hold at counter" staging.

**Fix**: Add a rootPath key at t≈5.72 (just before the nun cut, e.g., t=5.700) with the full counter-pose values (`anchorWorld:[690,925]`, `headingDeg:180`, `facing:-1`, `zone:"counter"`, etc.). The transition to walk-start values would then occur at/across the cut rather than during the store tail. Alternatively, set `interp:"step"` on the t=4.4 key.

### D-2: contacts/holds tracks use smooth interp instead of step (MINOR)

**Location**: `contacts` track (`interp:"smooth"`, 7 keys) and `holds` track (`interp:"smooth"`, 6 keys).

**Issue**: The notes.md says these tracks "carry resolved intents at beat boundaries only; the resolver holds the last value between them." The word "holds" implies step interpolation. However, `interp:"smooth"` means numeric leaves (specifically `ContactIntent.targetWorld` arrays and `HoldState.*.sinceSeconds` numbers) would be smoothly interpolated between sparse keys. For example:

- `contacts.feet.left.targetWorld` smoothly slides from [676,1280] (t=0) to [516,1280] (t=5.733) over 5.733s, even though the foot is planted at [676,1280] during that entire period.
- `holds.gaze.sinceSeconds` smoothly interpolates from 16.333 (t=16.333) to 21.833 (t=21.833), producing meaningless intermediate "since" timestamps like 19.1.

Boolean/string/enum fields correctly step per the API's "Only numeric leaves interpolate" rule, so the structural intents (active, kind, anchor, hold, locked, planted) are correct. The issue is limited to the advisory numeric fields.

**Impact**: Low — the rig uses `lowerKeys`/`hands`/`gaze` tracks for authoritative positions, not `contacts`/`holds`. But it's a semantic mismatch that could confuse downstream consumers.

**Fix**: Change `contacts` and `holds` tracks to `interp:"step"`.

## Visual concerns (render verification required, not blockers)

### V-1: Half-step-back foot slide (cue 4)

**Location**: `lowerKeys` track, t=15.3 → t=16.333.

**Data**: Both feet smoothly slide from y=1280 to y=1310 (30px over ~1s) while `planted:true` throughout. No foot-lift phase is encoded. The left foot traverses 1280→1295→1305→1310 through keys at t=15.3/15.667/16.0/16.333; the right foot traverses 1280→1285→1310→1310.

**Concern**: The acceptance envelope requires `contactErrorWorld <= 2.0 world px` for planted feet with "no stance sliding beyond rounding tolerance." A 30px slide while planted violates this in principle. However: (a) the character is fully off-screen in `work-drum` per scene-layout's visibility verdict; (b) feet are below the counter clip y=1110 in all shots; (c) this models a deliberate "shuffling back" rather than an unintended slip.

**Disposition**: Flag for coordinator. Consider whether the rig should interpret this as a foot-lift/re-plant or as a deliberate ground-contact slide. If the former, insert an intermediate key with `planted:false` and nonzero `clearance` for at least one foot.

### V-2: Gaze track vs holds track inconsistency (t=20.867)

**Location**: `gaze` track at t=20.867 has `lock:false` (transitioning from washer-gaze to tray area). But the sparse `holds` track (key at t=16.333 with `gaze.locked:true`, next key at t=21.833) holds `gaze.locked=true` through this period via step interpolation.

**Concern**: The gaze track (authoritative) says she unlocks gaze at the quiet→tray cut, but the advisory holds track says she remains locked. If a downstream consumer reads holds.gaze for display logic, it would show "locked" when the actual gaze is transitioning.

**Disposition**: Minor inconsistency. The gaze track is authoritative per API.md ("contacts/holds are resolved intents, not measured rig output"). Add a holds key at t=20.867 with `gaze.locked:false` to align, or accept the sparsity.

### V-3: Cue-6 planted foot micro-drift

**Location**: `lowerKeys` track, t=25.8 → t=27.0.

**Data**: Both feet shift 3px in x (left 676→673, right 704→707) while `planted:true`, modeling the cue-6 weight-back's effect on stance width. Rate: ~2px/s or ~0.07px/frame.

**Disposition**: At or below rounding tolerance. Acceptable pending render check. The feet are below counter clip y=1110 regardless.
