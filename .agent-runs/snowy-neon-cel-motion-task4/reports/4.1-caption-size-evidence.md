## Measurements — PIL + Kanit-Regular.ttf

### Methodology

- Font: Kanit-Regular.ttf loaded via PIL `ImageFont.truetype()` at exact pixel sizes
- Text widths: `font.getbbox(text)` → `bbox[2] - bbox[0]`
- Usable width: 1080 × (1 − 2×0.07) = **928.8 px** (7% side margins per CAPTION-NOTES.md)
- Phone scale: 390 / 1080 = 0.3611 linear scale factor

### All 12 cues — one-line fit

| Size | Longest cue | Width (px) | Margin remaining | All fit one line |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 19.2 px | Cue 12 | 488 | 441 px | **Yes** — all 12 cues |
| 22.4 px | Cue 12 | 566 | 363 px | **Yes** — all 12 cues |

Both sizes comfortably fit all cues in a single line.

### Sample cues 3, 5, 12 — detail

| Cue | Text | 19.2 px width | 22.4 px width |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 3 | งานของฉันมีแค่รับเหรียญ ใส่ผ้า | 241 px | 276 px |
| 5 | ดึก ๆ ร้านเงียบจนได้ยินเหรียญตกกระทบถาด | 341 px | 390 px |
| 12 | คืนนี้ฉันวางเหรียญ แล้วเฝ้ามองประตูหลัง อยากรู้ว่าเขามาจากไหน | 488 px | 566 px |

### Thai tone mark heights

| Size | Plain chars (กน) | With marks (ก็เสร็จ) | Full cue 12 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 19.2 px | 11 px | 17 px | 22 px |
| 22.4 px | 12 px | 19 px | 24 px |

Tone marks add 6–11 px above the baseline at both sizes. At 22.4 px, the total line height with marks (24 px) gives more room for the combining diacritical marks above vowels.

### Phone-scale (390 px viewport)

| Size | Effective font px | Above 6 px minimum | Tone mark effective height |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 19.2 px → | **6.93 px** | Yes (barely) | ~6.1 px with marks |
| 22.4 px → | **8.09 px** | Yes | ~7.0 px with marks |

The 19.2 px option passes the 6 px minimum but with only 0.93 px margin. The 22.4 px option has a 2.09 px margin. The index.html comment specifically chose 22.4 px because 19.2 px did not "restore Thai tone-mark rows at 390 px phone scale."

### Safe-band placement

The renderer positions captions at `bottom: 8.168%` of the viewport (index.html), which at 1920 px height places the bottom edge at y ≈ 1763. CAPTION-NOTES.md specifies the safe band at y 1724–1764. Both sizes fit within this band at both raster and phone scale — placement is size-independent.

## Evidence for each candidate

### 19.2 px — the accepted-v5 baseline

**Supporting:**
- CAPTION-NOTES.md calls this "the accepted EP1 v5 reference"
- original.json cites "compact 19.2 px baseline per coordinator message"
- Fits all 12 Jessica cues on one line with 441+ px margin
- Matches the "latest small subtitle style" direction (per CAPTION-NOTES.md reference to CAPTION-DIRECTION.md)
- Phone effective size 6.93 px passes the 6 px minimum

**Against:**
- index.html comment explicitly says 19.2 px was tested and rejected for phone scale because it doesn't "restore Thai tone-mark rows at 390 px"
- The referenced `evidence/34/capcmp/` phone-scale comparison that led to 22.4 px being chosen is not in this checkout for verification
- 6.93 px effective on phone is very small — tone marks at ~6 px are at the edge of legibility
- CAPTION-DIRECTION.md (the user's actual correction) is missing from the checkout

### 22.4 px — the current renderer default

**Supporting:**
- Current default in index.html `--cap-size: 2.0741vw`
- Explicitly chosen as "the smallest of {19.2, 22.4, 24} that restores Thai tone-mark rows at 390 px phone scale"
- Delivery document (`snowy-neon-ep1-delivery.th.md`) states 22.4 px for both editions
- 8.09 px effective on phone provides comfortable tone-mark legibility
- Previous render/delivery used this size (v2 delivery)

**Against:**
- CAPTION-NOTES.md doesn't mention 22.4 px as the accepted size; it recommends 19.2 px
- Contradicts original.json's "19.2 px per coordinator message"
- Larger than the "latest small" direction implies

## Unresolved items

1. **Missing CAPTION-DIRECTION.md** — The user's actual instruction to "unobtrusive subtitles at the accepted v5 scale" (paraphrased by CAPTION-NOTES.md) is not available for direct reading. This is the strongest evidence that could resolve the conflict.
2. **Missing evidence/34/capcmp/** — The phone-scale comparison images that led to the 22.4 px decision in index.html are not in the checkout. Without them, the "tone marks not restored at 19.2 px" claim cannot be independently verified against the renderer's actual output.
3. **Missing evidence/33/caption-size-check.json** — The PIL width measurements cited by CAPTION-NOTES.md are not in the checkout. The measurements in this report reproduce equivalent data.
4. **Two documents say 19.2 px, two say 22.4 px** — The plan correctly identified this as a conflict requiring user resolution. No single authoritative user approval was found for either size.
5. **jessica.srt unchanged** — Confirmed byte-for-byte match. No timing or text edits.

## Methodology notes

- All text measurements used PIL `ImageFont.truetype()` with the project's `Kanit-Regular.ttf` at the exact specified pixel sizes
- Phone-scale rendering used PIL at the proportionally scaled font size (19.2 × 390/1080 = 6.93, 22.4 × 390/1080 = 8.09)
- Contact sheets rendered on a dark background (#1e1838) matching the film's palette, with the renderer's translucent backing style approximated
- These are static PIL renders, not browser renders — actual CSS rendering in the browser may show slight differences in kerning, anti-aliasing, and subpixel rendering, especially at small sizes
